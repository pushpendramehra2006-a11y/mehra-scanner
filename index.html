📖 Mehra Scanner Guide

📷 Camera Scan: Phone camera se ek-ek karke clear photo lein aur direct PDF ya image me export karein.
📂 Gallery / PDF Import: Gallery se images ya puraane PDFs, DOCX aur Excel sheets ko import karke inspect aur convert karein.
🔄 Auto Rotation: Screen rotate hone par layout automatically screen ke hisab se adjust hoga.
⚡ Offline Support: Is app ko chalane ke liye internet ki zaroorat nahi hai. Sabhi tools offline chalenge.
🌙 Light / Dark Mode: Upar diye button se apni marzi se theme switch kar sakte hain.
